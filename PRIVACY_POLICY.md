# Privacy Policy for Domain Bookmark Organizer

**Last Updated:** July 26, 2026

## 1. Overview
The "Domain Bookmark Organizer" extension is designed to help users organize their Chrome bookmarks by domain name inside specified folders. We value your privacy, and this Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled.

## 2. No Data Collection or Tracking
- **Zero Data Collection:** We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal information, browsing history, website content, or tracking metrics.
- **No Analytics:** This extension contains no third-party tracking, analytics, or external API calls.
- **Local Processing Only:** All operations (reading bookmarks, extracting domain names, creating sub-folders, and moving bookmarks) are processed entirely locally on your device.

## 3. Permissions Justification
The extension requests only the minimum necessary permissions to perform its intended functionality:

- **`bookmarks`**: Required strictly to read your bookmarks hierarchy, extract domain names from bookmarked URLs, create new domain folders, and re-organize the links upon your direct interaction (clicking "Organize").
- **`storage`**: Required strictly to save your preferred interface language (English or Arabic) locally in your browser so you do not have to re-select it every time.

## 4. Third-Party Services
This extension does not share, send, or transfer any data to third-party services, servers, or external networks.

## 5. Developer & Support Contact
If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding privacy or the functionality of this extension, please reach out through the following channels:

- **Developer Website:** https://www.mwmworld.com
